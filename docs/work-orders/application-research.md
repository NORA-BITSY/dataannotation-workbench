# DataAnnotation application research work order

**Owner** Product Pat  
**Scope** Public DataAnnotation opportunities and pre-assessment preparation  
**Initiated** November 7, 2026

## Decision to make

Identify the highest-paying track Pat can credibly pursue now, the best fallback tracks, and the concrete experience or credentials required for higher-paying work. Produce an application sequence and a practice plan he can use before taking assessments independently.

## Work steps

1. **Verify the platform and application routes.** Identify the official domain, current FAQ, role pages, public postings, and application entry points. Separate paid project tracks, recruiting pages, credentials, and platform-wide rules. Verify whether rates are advertised starting rates, ceilings, or ranges, and whether project volume is guaranteed.
2. **Inventory the public opportunities.** Record each publicly advertised track, its pay, representative work, screening requirements, location restrictions where stated, and application link. Capture specialty and language families and call out anything visible only after signup. Reconcile conflicts across official sources.
3. **Map Pat’s evidence to requirements.** Use current relevant records if available. Compare established finance and programming experience with each role. Separate hard credential gates, evidence Pat can supply, gaps he can study, and gaps that require actual qualifications. Prioritize compensation, eligibility, application effort, and likely overlap with his skills.
4. **Verify assessment rules and disclosed content.** Check official guidance for sequence, timing, attempts, allowed resources, and what each track tests. Label any inferred preparation topics. Use official examples where public and original drills. Keep this work outside any live assessment and leave actual assessment answers to Pat.
5. **Build the preparation packet.** Include a short first-session plan, role-specific study checklists, original timed drills, answer keys, and a scoring rubric. Put the highest-value viable roles first. Identify a practical route toward higher rates and when further preparation makes sense.
6. **Deliver application next steps.** Give official links, order of operations, factual experience bullets, and the qualifications or documentation to check before attempting any one-time screen. Keep application decisions and live assessment work with Pat.

## Completion criteria

- Each current public track is accounted for, including out-of-scope or credential-gated families.
- The $150/hour claim has a traceable public source or an explicit verification gap.
- Recommendations distinguish verified requirements from preparation judgment and are grounded in Pat’s experience.
- The packet contains drills he can start immediately, with self-checks and a clear boundary around the live assessment.
- Each recommended track has a named first action and an official entry point.
