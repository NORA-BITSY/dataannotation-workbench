Take either form in 20 minutes. Use only its fictional evidence packet. Submit a verdict from A, B, tie or both bad, a short rationale citing the supplied record IDs, and a customer-ready rewrite of at most 40 words. Recheck the constraints before you finish.

## GEN-A · Conditional refund

P1 says an operator cancellation can qualify for a refund of the rental fee after review. P2 excludes the $9 third-party booking fee. R1 confirms the operator canceled and the rental fee was $80. R2 says review is pending.

A says “We already refunded your full $89.” B says “Your $80 rental fee is eligible for review. The $9 booking fee is excluded, and the refund decision is still pending.”

## GEN-B · Confirmed arrival

P1 says only a confirmed arrival may be presented as booked. P2 requires photo ID at check-in. R1 marks Tuesday 9 a.m. tentative. R2 confirms Wednesday 11 a.m. and marks Tuesday unavailable.

A says “You're booked Tuesday at 9. Bring a photo ID.” B says “You're booked Wednesday at 11. Check-in needs no ID.”
