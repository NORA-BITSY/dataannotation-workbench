## First practice drill

Take 12 minutes before opening any live assessment. A fictional boat-service business budgeted **120 jobs at $250**, with **$100 variable cost per job** and **$12,000 fixed expenses**. It completed **105 jobs at $270**, with **$112 variable cost per job** and **$12,400 fixed expenses**.

1. Calculate budgeted and actual operating profit and the difference.
2. Bridge the difference into volume, price, variable unit cost, and fixed expense. Use budget contribution per job for volume, and actual volume for price and variable unit cost.
3. Write two sentences for management. Name the largest driver and one data point to investigate before explaining its cause.

Check whether the four effects sum to the total. Use the main prep packet for the longer practice round, self-scoring, and solution keys. [Role Fit and Assessment Prep](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_6a88005e4da881918bb17faeee87a76b&account_id=personal)
