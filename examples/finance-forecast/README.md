# Applied lab · Forecast and cash review · v1

**Practice time** 75 minutes. Use only the original fictional records in [source-records.csv](source-records.csv) and the flawed [draft model](draft-model.csv). A spreadsheet, CSV editor or calculator is allowed for this lab. The company is a fictional marine service business. All amounts are in whole dollars. Treat authoritative records as controlling. Exclude superseded and outside-period amounts.

## Assignment

1. Rebuild the October budget and actual operating profit. Reconcile their difference using a four-factor bridge. Use budget contribution for volume, actual units for price and variable-cost effects, and favorable signs as positive.
2. Build the November base and downside operating-profit forecasts. Keep cash separate. Calculate each scenario's ending cash before financing and minimum funding to reach the floor. Collections and operating payments are supplied independently of accrual profit. Assume the minimum applies at month-end, and that financing, if any, increases cash dollar for dollar.
3. Audit the five rows of the draft. Identify the material differences with exact source IDs, including the version of capex and the timing of the unsettled receipt. Do not double count the December collection.
4. Deliver a one-page management note and a calculation trail with formulas. Tie the operating bridge to the independent profit endpoints. Tie cash again as opening cash plus net movement and show the minimum funding restores the cash floor. State a specific action and an unresolved assumption, without inventing a reason for the volume movement.

Round to the nearest whole dollar only at output. These inputs already yield integer dollars. Use `outputs.csv` or a spreadsheet with metric, scenario, value, formula and source IDs. The answer key and verification code are separate from this packet.
