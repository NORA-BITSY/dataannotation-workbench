# Finance and FP&A diagnostic · v1

Each form is a fresh 55-minute practice attempt. Use 25 minutes for the profit bridge, 15 for cash, 10 for the management note and five for checking. You may use a blank spreadsheet or calculator for practice. Work unaided and choose one form before looking at any solutions. Submit your formulas, completed bridge, cash calculation and note. Dollar inputs in profit tables are dollars. Cash packets are in thousands of dollars.

For all forms, use budget contribution for volume and actual units for price and variable-cost effects. Present favorable effects as positive. Calculate ending cash before financing and the minimum funding needed to meet the cash floor. Round dollars to the nearest dollar and cash to the nearest $1k.

## FIN-A · Marine parts distributor

| Input | Budget | Actual |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Units | 1,000 | 900 |
| Price per unit | $100 | $105 |
| Variable cost per unit | $55 | $57 |
| Fixed expense | $30,000 | $32,000 |

1. Calculate both operating profits, their difference, and the volume, price, variable-cost and fixed-cost bridge. Show it ties to the difference.
2. Cash records show beginning cash 42, settled collections 70, cash operating payments 64 and equipment payment 25. The cash floor is 30. An extra customer deposit of 8 is expected on day 31, after this month. A draft includes it this month. Give the correct result and identify the relevant timing evidence.
3. In two sentences, summarize the profit movement and liquidity action. The records do not explain the unit decline.
